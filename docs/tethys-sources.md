# Tethys mapped surface provenance

Prepared by `scripts/prepare-saturn-moons.mjs`; rebuild with `node scripts/prepare-saturn-moons.mjs --rebuild`, or add `--download-pinned` to retrieve pinned originals. Originals and normalized masters stay under ignored `output/surface-originals/`.

[Full-resolution mission map](https://planetarymaps.usgs.gov/mosaic/Tethys_Cassini_mosaic_global_293m.tif): 11520 × 5760, grayscale, nominal 292.5063 m/pixel. SHA-256 `40723c3ebfaaa52a657827b37309bc84234811db1cf26a21d086e26aba2b3640`. This is mapped Cassini ISS imagery, not a terrain model. Dione includes Voyager gap-filling. Native source resolution varies across the mosaic; seams and image illumination remain visible. Preview 512 × 256, medium 1024 × 512, and near 4096 × 2048 JPEG levels are downsampled from the source, with no upscaling, sharpening, colorization, or invented detail.

The source simple cylindrical map is north-up, planetocentric, east-positive −180..180°. Dione and Tethys GeoTIFF projection parameters are verified against their pinned detached USGS labels. Their label SHA-256 checksums are retained in the generated manifest. Native map projection radius 536300 m is preserved as source metadata; it does not overwrite the simulation radius. Normalized TIFF masters and JSON sidecars record checksums and coordinate conventions for optional detail tiling.

[JPL satellite physical parameters](https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/sats/phys_par/) provide GM 41.21353 km³/s² and mean radius 531100 m (SAT441; checked 2026-10-24). Mass is computed as GM/G. [JPL mean elements](https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/sats/elem/sep.html) supply circularized initial separation, rounded inclination and local Laplace plane. Initial phase is illustrative, not an epoch ephemeris. Body-fixed radii and analytic rotation are generated directly from the pinned [NAIF pck00011.tpc](https://naif.jpl.nasa.gov/pub/naif/generic_kernels/pck/pck00011.tpc), independent of orbital dynamics.

Measured terrain exists: [Weirich et al. 2025 PDS SPC shape and assessment bundle](https://doi.org/10.26033/hpv0-eh61) includes scientific global radius products. The archive returned Cloudflare access challenges during this preparation, so no measured height grid was downloaded or decoded. The shipped product uses the PCK ellipsoid with no radial height map. This is an integration/access limitation, not a claim that public terrain data do not exist. No brightness-derived, synthetic, or illustrated elevation is used. The newer Mimas stereo DEM discussed in [LPSC 2025 abstract 2435](https://www.hou.usra.edu/meetings/lpsc2025/pdf/2435.pdf) is also a research lead, not an acquired raster.
