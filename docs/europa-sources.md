# Europa surface preparation

The checked-in Europa derivatives are generated by
`scripts/prepare-europa-surface.mjs --rebuild` from the ignored original
`output/surface-originals/europa-usgs-full.tif`.

## Verified source

The source is the USGS Astrogeology [Europa Voyager - Galileo SSI Global
Mosaic 500m](https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/europa_voyager_galileo_ssi_global_mosaic_500m).
The official full GeoTIFF is 19,631×9,816, 8-bit single-band imagery with a
SHA-256 of:

`a323f0c9ccb47d5af9902ea8297fe81f9a9708795645b80801f103c3f7c9a624`

The USGS ISIS label records a Simple Cylindrical projection on a sphere of
radius 1,562,089.9658 m, planetocentric latitude, positive-west longitude in
the 0..360 domain, center longitude 180°, upper-left projected origin
(-4,907,750.3455436, 2,453,875.1727718) m, and 499.97456657942 m pixels.
The runtime uses the canonical simulation radius 1,560,800 m.

## Longitude normalization

The source is not corrected with a positive-west horizontal flip alone. Its
prime center is 180W, so the preparation reverses the positive-west axis and
shifts the half-raster seam first. The result is east-positive −180..180,
planetocentric latitude, north-up, pixel-centered equirectangular imagery.

## Relief policy

This product is mapped imagery, not a DEM. Its pixel values are never used as
elevation. The accompanying `.height.bin` files are an explicit deterministic
procedural field independent of the source pixels, encoded as little-endian
unsigned 16-bit samples with `height = -96 + sample × 0.5 m`. The range is
deliberately bounded to −96..95.5 m and is not a claim about Europa's measured
topography or ice-shell shape.

## Derived levels

The full source supports three downsampled levels: preview 512×256, medium
1024×512, and near 2048×1024. No level is upsampled beyond the source. Color
derivatives are JPEG quality 88 with 4:4:4 chroma; all output SHA-256 values
are recorded in `assets/surfaces/europa-manifest.json`.
