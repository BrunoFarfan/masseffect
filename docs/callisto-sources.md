# Callisto surface preparation

The checked-in derivatives are generated by
`node scripts/prepare-callisto-surface.mjs --rebuild --offline` from the
ignored originals in `output/surface-originals/`. Refresh the source cache with
`--download-pinned`; rebuild verifies both pinned SHA-256 values.

## Verified source

The source is the USGS Astrogeology [Callisto Galileo/Voyager Global Mosaic
1km](https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/callisto_galileo_voyager_global_mosaic_1km),
assembled from NASA Voyager and Galileo SSI imagery. The GeoTIFF is
15,138×7,569, 8-bit single-band, with a 1,000.0051966711 m/pixel Simple
Cylindrical map. Its companion ISIS label records planetocentric latitude,
positive-west longitude in 0..360, center longitude 180°, upper-left projected
origin (-7,569,039.3336036, 3,784,519.6668018) m, and source radius
2,409,300.0488 m. The source SHA-256 is recorded in the manifest and script.

The simulation uses canonical `R = 2,410,300 m`; the difference from the
source metadata radius is explicit in the manifest rather than silently
substituted.

## Orientation and relief policy

The source positive-west axis is reversed and its half-raster seam shifted
using the label metadata, producing east-positive -180..180,
planetocentric-latitude, north-up, pixel-centered equirectangular imagery.
This is a metadata-derived geotransform operation, not visual alignment.
Color remains mapped imagery: no legend or map decoration is baked into the
derivatives, and brightness is never used as elevation.

The source is not a DEM. Medium and near levels therefore use an explicitly
bounded deterministic synthetic field independent of image pixels. The field
is evaluated from a 3D unit direction vector, so it is seamless at longitude
and both poles. Samples are little-endian uint16 with
`height = -256 + sample × 0.5 m`; actual decoded minima and maxima and hashes
are recorded in `assets/surfaces/callisto-manifest.json`.

## Derived levels

Preview is color-only at 512×256. Medium is 1024×512 and near is 2048×1024;
all are downsampled from the 15,138×7,569 source (never upsampled beyond its
dimensions). Colors are JPEG quality 88 with 4:4:4 chroma. Original scientific
source files remain ignored under `output/surface-originals/` and are not
runtime assets.
