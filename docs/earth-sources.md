# Earth surface preparation

The checked-in Earth derivatives are generated by
`node scripts/prepare-earth-surface.mjs --rebuild` from the ignored originals
in `output/surface-originals/`.

The height source is NOAA NCEI ETOPO 2022 v1 60s, fetched as a bounded
10-sample stride from the ERDDAP grid. The pinned CSV is 2160×1080, with
south-to-north geodetic latitude rows and 0..360° east longitude columns.
Its SHA-256 is `efac6cf1798875d2dd175fb2a73d32bd25832f0e1aad2db74701b8ddafec3690`.
The source vertical coordinate is EGM2008 orthometric height, not ellipsoidal
height. The preparation therefore records an explicit datum approximation:
geoid undulations are not downloaded or corrected, and the manifest does not
claim exact WGS84 radial heights.

The color source is NASA Earth Observatory Blue Marble Next Generation,
January 2004, `world.200401.3x5400x2700.jpg` (SHA-256
`99f5faad74efe985fbf1714c8be7296ca9999759a1215b65f99b7f1df278dde5`). It is
cloud-free mapped color only; no height is inferred from its pixels.

For each output pixel, target planetocentric latitude is converted to the
corresponding WGS84 geodetic latitude before bilinear sampling of both source
products. Rows are north-up and longitude is east-positive −180..180. Heights
encode the WGS84 ellipsoid radial surface plus ETOPO z relative to the
canonical 6,371,000 m Earth radius, as little-endian uint16 with a −30,000 m
offset and 1 m scale. Bathymetry remains in the height data; the renderer
uses the reference ellipsoid for the visible ocean surface rather
than presenting undersea geometry as visible water.

The generated levels are `preview` 512×256, `medium` 1024×512, and `near`
2048×1024. Their output hashes, dimensions, radial min/max, and validation
samples for Everest, the Andes, and the Pacific are recorded in
`assets/surfaces/earth-manifest.json`.
