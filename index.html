Scale & history

Distances and physics use real SI quantities. Small bodies are enlarged for visibility. Trails record actual motion, never predicted ellipses.

« rewinds; Ⅱ pauses either direction; › resumes forward. History is bounded, and trails clear during rewind. Merges can be restored from recordings, not analytically reversed. Fast playback may reach the history boundary quickly.
